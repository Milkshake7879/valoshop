Stay logged in

Save your Riot session cookie in this browser and your store opens instantly, for weeks at a time. Set it up once, on a computer.

  1. Log in with Riot and tick Stay signed in on Riot's page.
  2. In Chrome, Opera or Edge, open this Riot page.
  3. Press ⌘⌥I (or F12) → Application → Cookies → auth.riotgames.com.
  4. Copy the value of the ssid row and paste it below. Do the same on your phone to stay logged in there too.

Treat it like a password: anyone with it is logged into your Riot account. It stays in this browser, and this site's server passes it to Riot without keeping it. Signing in to another account ends it.

Still skipped past Riot's login? Sign out of Riot, then log in again.
